Privacy Policy

Applies to: Unicorn Trail, Rainbow Kingdom and Chain Reaction by LokaKids · Last updated: 28 November 2026

The short version

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. Our games are made for children. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the games save a few small items on your own device only: stars, best scores, unlocked levels and settings such as music and vibration. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset progress, or by clearing the app's storage.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, locations, photos, contacts or device identifiers.
  • We do not show advertising or use third-party advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not load fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers. Everything the games need is delivered from our own website and cached on the device so they also work offline.
  • We do not use the microphone or camera. The narrator in Rainbow Kingdom uses the speech voices already built into your device and does not record audio.

Children

These games are designed to be safe for children. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share with advertisers or other companies. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us with any questions.

Permissions

The games may use the device's vibration feature for gentle feedback (you can turn this off in Settings). No other special permissions are requested.

Changes

If this policy ever changes we will update this page and the date above.
