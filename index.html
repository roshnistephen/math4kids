How to play

Draw a path

Drag your finger or use the arrow keys from the unicorn to the castle.

Collect sparkles

Every sparkle you pass through is worth +10 points.

Avoid obstacles

Bumping into an obstacle costs −5 points, so go around them.

Reach the castle

Get home for a +20 bonus and up to three ⭐ stars.
